Privacy Policy – Color Extractor

Effective Date: January 2026

Color Extractor (“the App”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

1. Summary

Color Extractor is designed to extract HEX and RGB color values from images you choose or capture. Core color extraction is performed on your device. The App displays advertisements through Google’s advertising platform (AdMob), which may process certain data as described below.

2. Information You Provide

The App does not require registration. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or similar account details to use the core features.

3. Camera and Photo Library

The App may request access to your camera or photo library so you can select or capture an image to extract color values.

Color extraction is performed locally on your device. We do not operate our own servers to receive, store, or upload your images for this purpose.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. When ads are shown, Google and its partners may collect and process certain information to deliver and measure advertising, which may include:

  • Device information (such as device type, OS version, and general technical identifiers)
  • Advertising-related identifiers where permitted by platform settings and applicable law (such as Apple’s advertising identifier where available, subject to your device settings and any consent prompts)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (typically at a coarse level)
  • Ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks)

Google may use this information to show personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your settings, platform policies, and applicable regulations. For details on how Google uses data, see Google’s Privacy & Terms: https://policies.google.com/privacy

You can manage ad tracking preferences on iOS through your device settings (for example, Apple’s privacy and tracking controls). Depending on your region, the App may present consent or preference options required for ads.

5. Analytics and Other Third-Party SDKs (Non-ad)

Beyond advertising, we do not integrate third-party analytics SDKs in order to track your usage of the App for our own analytics purposes.

6. Data Retention

We do not maintain our own user database for the core color extraction features. Data processed by Google for advertising is handled according to Google’s policies and retention practices.

7. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information in a way that concerns you, please contact us. Note that advertising services may have their own policies and requirements; we aim to comply with applicable rules for apps directed to children.

8. International Users

Advertising providers may process data in various countries in accordance with their policies and applicable law.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
